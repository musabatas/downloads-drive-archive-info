Personal macOS utility

Downloads Drive Archive

A private, single-user automation that archives completed files from the Mac's Downloads folder to the owner's Google Drive.

The utility checks for completed downloads every 15 minutes. Local files remain for at least seven days after their first verified upload. Changed files begin a new retention period. Before removing an eligible local file, the utility checks the Drive copy again.

Google access is limited to the drive.file permission: files created by or explicitly used with this application. The utility is not offered as a hosted service and does not accept other users.
